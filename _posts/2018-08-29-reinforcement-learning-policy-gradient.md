1. Policy Gradinet Theorem
2. REINFORCE with eligibility trace(episodic, continuing)
3. Actor-Critic with eligibility trace(episodic, continuing)

There are two extra three papers that I prepare
1. Deterministic Policy Gradinet Theorem (DPG Theorem)
2. Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient(DDPG = DQN+DPG)
3. Soft Actor-Critic
